# Code of conduct

We want Taveloom to be a friendly place to contribute, whatever your background or experience.

**Do:** be respectful, assume good intent, give specific and kind feedback, accept critique of your work without taking it personally.

**Don't:** harass, insult, discriminate, publish others' private information, or make personal attacks.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules and may block repeat offenders. Report problems to the
maintainer, EFEDEV, through the profile listed on the repository. This policy is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant.
