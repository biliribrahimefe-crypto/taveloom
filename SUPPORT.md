# Support

Taveloom is a community-supported beta. There is no paid support or SLA.

## Get help

1. Read the troubleshooting list in [docs/installation/windows.md](docs/installation/windows.md#if-a-phone-cannot-connect)
   and [docs/installation/android.md](docs/installation/android.md#troubleshooting).
2. Search [existing issues](https://github.com/biliribrahimefe-crypto/taveloom/issues).
3. Open a new issue with the **Bug report** template. Include: Taveloom versions (Windows and Android), Windows build,
   phone model and Android version, what you did, what you expected, what happened, and any error text.
   Remove IP addresses and device names you do not want to share. **Never post pairing QR codes or screenshots of them.**

## Security problems

Do not open a public issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Not supported yet

Windows 10, iOS, running the PC app as a service, connecting across different networks or the internet.
