## What helps most
- The exact error text and the moment it happened (pairing, connecting, pressing a control...).
- Whether the problem survives a restart of Taveloom on the PC and of the app on the phone.
- For connection problems: Taveloom → Devices → *Connection troubleshooting* on the PC (copy its lines; they contain your private IP addresses, which you may blank out).

## Privacy of your report
Taveloom collects no telemetry or logs for us, so nothing is sent automatically; we only know what you write. Remove IP addresses and device names you do not want to share.
